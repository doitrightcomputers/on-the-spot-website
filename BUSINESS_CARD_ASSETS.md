## Printing Instructions

### Recommended Printer
- Use a professional print shop (Vistaprint, Minted, local print shop, etc.)
- Request: **Standard Business Cards** (3.5" x 2")
- Stock: **Premium cardstock** (14pt or thicker) with optional gloss or matte finish
- Finish: **Full-color, both sides**

### File Preparation
1. Download both PNG files (front and back)
2. Upload to your print shop's website
3. Select "3.5 x 2 inches" dimensions
4. Choose cardstock quality (recommend premium or luxury)
5. Order quantity (minimum 250 cards recommended)

### Design Specifications
- **Bleed:** 0.125" on all sides (already included in design)
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI (suitable for professional printing)
- **Color Mode:** RGB (will be converted to CMYK by printer)
- **Font:** Serif (Playfair Display style) for elegance
